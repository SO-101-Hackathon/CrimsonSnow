All episodes

Same every time

  • All four places are filled, except in round 3, where the battery is alone.
  • The arm only ever picks up the battery. After the episode, take the battery out of the box and put it where the next episode says.
  • Put the battery straight in its place, square to the arm, unless the episode says otherwise.
  • In one episode in four the battery sits slightly off: turned about 15° left or right, or 1 cm closer or farther. "Left" and "right" are as the operator sees them from behind the arm.
  • The box stays taped where it is. The cameras do not move.
  • Start and end with the arm at rest. Go straight to the battery, lift, drop it low over the box.
  • If an episode goes wrong, redo it before ticking.

Four objects on four places can be arranged in 24 ways. One pass goes through all 24 with one swap of two neighbours each time, and the last arrangement is one swap from the first, so the passes run on without a full reset. Rounds 1, 2, 4 and 5 are two passes each. Round 3 is the battery alone, twice per place. The battery ends up at each place 50 times.
