Battery Fix Plan

The model is already accurate when the stick lies straight on its spot. Tonight we teach it the small slips that happen when someone puts the stick down by hand, without weakening the normal cases: 132 new recordings in one session, 12 of each change (turned left 20°, turned right 20°, moved up 2 cm, moved down 2 cm) and 84 straight, so straight stays 80 % of everything the model learns from.

What fails, and what we record for it

What failsMost likely whyWhat we record
Stick turned left or right: it does not even try. It has only ever seen the stick lying straight. 24 episodes: 12 turned left 20° and 12 turned right 20°, three of each at every place.
Grabbing below the middle of the stick: it gets stuck. At the very top: it sometimes fails. When the stick is a little off its spot, the arm still goes to the usual spot and closes on the end of the stick. 24 episodes: 12 moved up 2 cm (away from the arm) and 12 moved down 2 cm (toward the arm), three of each at every place. Always close on the middle of the stick.
Big changes (45°, across, far off the spot). Too far from anything it has seen. Left out on purpose. For the demo we put the stick on its spot. Teaching big changes would take data away from the normal cases.

In every new recording come down from straight above, so the wrist camera keeps the stick in view until the jaws close.

The evening, in order

  1. 10 minPrepare. Draw a 20° line on a piece of paper to lay next to the stick while placing it; take it away before you record. Mark the four spots on the mat with tape, with a short line for the stick's direction (for the demo too). In SenseCraft, continue recording into battery sample, or record into a new dataset and merge the two afterwards.
  2. 20 minExam the current model (E1 to E20 below, column "Now"). SenseCraft closed, then so try --sensecraft "battery sample". Type s E7 or f E7 stuck low in the terminal and tap the result here too.
  3. 2.5 hRecord all 132 in one session on the Battery Fix Recordings page: 12 setups recorded seven times each, with single straight recordings in between.
  4. 10 minCheck and merge. Watch five new episodes and delete any fumbled grab. Merge with battery sample: 108 + 132 = 240 episodes (80 % straight), named battery v2.
  5. overnightTrain ACT on battery v2, about 89,000 steps (more data needs more steps: 40,000 was right for 108 episodes).
  6. SundayExam the new model (column "New"): so try --sensecraft "battery v2". Demo with it only if it does at least as well on E1 to E12, the normal cases. Otherwise demo with the current model.

So the normal cases stay strong

  • Train on old and new together, never on the new ones alone. SenseCraft always trains from zero on the dataset you give it, so merge first.
  • Straight stays 80 %: 108 old + 84 new straight = 192 of 240 episodes. Single straight recordings sit between the setups, so even if you stop early it never drops below 79.4 %.
  • Same movement as in the old recordings: start at rest, come down from straight above, close on the middle of the stick, lift, drop low over the box, back to rest. For a turned stick, turn the wrist to line up with it before closing.
  • One person drives the leader arm for all 132, so the movement style stays the same.
  • Keep everything else as it was: cameras, box, mat, light, and the duck, car and TNT on the mat.
  • The exam decides. The current model stays the demo model unless the new one is at least as good on the normal cases.
