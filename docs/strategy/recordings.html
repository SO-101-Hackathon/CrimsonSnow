Battery Fix Recordings

132 recordings in one session: 12 setups recorded seven times each, with single straight recordings in between. At the end there are 12 of each change (turned left and right 20°, moved up and down 2 cm) and, with the 108 old episodes, exactly 80 % straight. If you stop early, it never drops below 79.4 %.

Every setup, in this order

  1. Straight
  2. Turned left 20°
  3. Turned right 20°
  4. Straight
  5. Up 2 cm
  6. Down 2 cm
  7. Straight

Between setups come single straight recordings with one swap each. Up is away from the arm, down is toward it; left and right are as the operator sees them from behind the arm.

When all 132 are ticked

  1. Watch five of the new episodes in SenseCraft and delete any with a fumbled grab.
  2. Merge them with battery sample: 108 + 132 = 240 episodes, 80 % straight. Name it battery v2.
  3. Train ACT on battery v2 for about 89,000 steps (more data needs more steps: 40,000 was right for 108 episodes).
  4. Run the exam on the Battery Fix Plan and demo with the model that does best on the normal cases.
